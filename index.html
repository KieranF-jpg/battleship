          Tips & tricks

          Battleship looks like luck, but good captains sink the fleet in far fewer shots. Here's how.

          1. Hunt on a checkerboard

          Every ship covers at least 2 squares, so firing only at the gold squares below is guaranteed to find every ship in at most 50 shots instead of 100. Once the 2-square ship is sunk, widen the gap to every third square.

          2. Start in the middle

          More ship positions pass through the centre than the edges. This heatmap shows how many ways the fleet can cover each square on an empty board: brighter means a ship is more likely to be there.

          3. After a hit, finish the job

          Fire at the four squares around the hit. When you find a second hit you know the direction: keep going along that line, and when you reach water, go back and finish the other end.

          4. Watch for ships side by side

          If a line of hits is longer than the ship you just sank, two ships are touching. Check the fleet list under the board to see which ships are still afloat.

          5. Skip gaps that are too small

          Keep track of what's left. If only the 5-square ship remains, don't fire into any gap of water shorter than 5 squares.

          6. Hide your fleet well

          Change your layout every game and avoid neat patterns. The Hard AI plays the odds, so edges and corners are where it looks last, and ships touching each other can confuse its follow-up shots.

          7. Know your enemy

          • Easy fires at random.
          • Medium hunts on a checkerboard and follows up hits.
          • Hard works out the odds for every square, like the heatmap above, after every shot.

          8. Learn from every battle

          After a game, choose Watch replay and turn on AI radar to see where the AI thought your ships were. Stats track your best accuracy and fewest shots to win.
